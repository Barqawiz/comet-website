Privacy policy

The short version: Comet the Shiba does not collect any personal data, and there is no server on the other end to send it to.

This policy covers the iPhone and iPad game Comet the Shiba, developed by Barqawiz. It applies to the app itself and to this website.

No personal data is collected

The game does not collect, store or transmit personal data. There are no accounts to create, no sign-up, no email capture, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. The app's App Store privacy label is Data Not Collected, and its bundled privacy manifest declares no collected data types and no tracking domains.

What is stored on your device

Your progress is saved locally with Apple's standard on-device storage (UserDefaults), and it never leaves the device. That means: which levels you have unlocked and completed, your best score, your points balance and which pals you own, your lifetime stats, your sound, music and haptics settings, and whether the rating card has already been shown.

None of it is synced to a server, because there is no server. Deleting the app deletes all of it. You can also erase it yourself at any time from Settings → Reset progress inside the game.

Game Center is optional

If you are signed in to Game Center, the game submits your best score to a leaderboard and reports achievements. That data is handled by Apple under Apple's privacy policy, not by me: I receive no identity information, no email address and no player details.

Game Center is entirely optional. Signed out, the game is fully playable and simply skips the leaderboard and achievements. You can sign out at any time in the iOS Settings app.

No purchases, no ads, no third-party code

There are no in-app purchases and no subscriptions, so no payment information is involved anywhere. There is no advertising. The app contains no third-party SDKs, analytics libraries, ad networks, attribution kits or middleware of any kind, only Apple's own frameworks.

Ratings and feedback

After finishing a region the game may ask, at most three times per install, how you are enjoying it. A high rating hands off to Apple's own review prompt. A lower one offers to open your mail app with a pre-filled message to me. Nothing is sent unless you press send yourself, and your star choice stays on your device.

If you do email me, I see whatever you write plus your email address, and I use it only to reply to you.

Children

Comet the Shiba is rated 4+ and suitable for all ages. Because it collects no data at all, it collects nothing from children either.

This website

This site is a set of static files hosted on GitHub Pages. It sets no cookies and runs no analytics. GitHub may log ordinary web-server request data as part of serving the pages; see GitHub's privacy statement. Web fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which receives the request for the font files.

Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes, the effective date at the top of the page changes with it.
