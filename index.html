How it plays

Everything lives in the jump.

Comet runs on a custom fixed-timestep engine — not a physics library — so the jump arc is the same on every device, every frame. The forgiving parts are deliberate.

Coyote time & jump buffering

Step off a ledge and you still get your jump. Press early into a landing and it fires the instant you touch down. Missed inputs stop being the reason you died.

Variable jump height

Tap for a hop, hold for the full 3.7-tile arc. Every gap in the game is authored against those limits, so a route is never a guess.

Chain your stomps

One pest is 100 points. Land the next before you touch ground and it doubles — up to 800. The greedy line and the safe line are rarely the same line.

Zoomies

Grab a bolt and Comet becomes untouchable for a few seconds — long enough to run straight through a spikeshell that has been bullying you for a minute.

Hazards tell you first

Crumble planks shake before they break. Dust geysers hiss before they erupt. Shardlings shimmer before their backs flare. Nothing kills you without a tell.

Landscape, thumbs or keys

Purpose-built touch controls with a dedicated iPad layout — and full hardware keyboard support whenever one is attached.

The journey

Three regions. Thirty locations. One doghouse.

Each region introduces its own hazards, keeps everything that came before it, and ends at a gate you have to fight your way through.

Levels 1–10

Greenward Coast

Sunny parks, moonlit caves and rooftop runs. Springs launch you seven tiles up, crumble planks give way underfoot, and hoppers arc toward you the moment you commit. The Citadel Warden guards the gate at the end.

  • Scuttlers
  • Hoppers
  • Spikeshells
  • Buzzers
Levels 11–20

Amber Highlands

A desert town under a gold sky. Tumbleweeds roll under real gravity — off ledges, into pits, straight at you — and duneswoopers circle their perch before diving at where you are standing. From level 16 the ground itself starts erupting.

  • Tumbleweeds
  • Duneswoopers
  • Highland Alpha
Levels 21–30

Starfall Reach

Frost fields under an aurora. Shardlings carry star-crystals that flare on a staggered clock — stomp them only while dark. Glimmermoths drift toward you and settle just above head height. Every earlier hazard comes back, colder.

  • Shardlings
  • Glimmermoths
  • Starfall Tyrant

Region gates

You don't out-jump a Warden. You out-bark it.

Every region ends in a duel. A BARK button appears, and the fight is a read — not a damage race.

Citadel Warden

Greenward Coast · 3 HP

Amber Highlands · 4 HP

Starfall Tyrant

Starfall Reach · Final duel

Face the charge

Stand your ground and let it commit. Backing off just resets the pattern.

Bark, or sidestep

Bark once the charge is underway and it trips. Dodge instead and it slams into the arena wall — same result, more nerve.

Stomp the head

+200 a hit while it's stunned, +1500 when it goes down — and the gate falls to reveal the doghouse.

The pet shop

Four more dogs. No money involved.

Points are earned by playing, banked as a personal best per route, and spent in the shop. Every pal handles identically — the physics never changes, only the dog does.

Total roster cost

A perfect save holds exactly 120,000 points — 3,000 from each of the thirty routes plus 10,000 for each region cleared. The four prices add up to precisely that, so the full roster lands on the last dog who earned it. A unit test fails if the numbers ever drift apart.
