Comet the Shiba

A pixel platformer for iPhone and iPad. Run, jump and stomp your way through 30 levels in three regions, and help one Shiba Inu get home.

Download on theApp Store

Free to download. Levels 1 and 2 are free, and one optional purchase unlocks the rest. No ads, no subscriptions. I made it on my own, and every sprite, sound and note is original.

How it plays

You run with the two pads and jump with the paw button. The movement engine is custom and runs at 120 Hz, so a jump behaves the same on every device. A few details worth knowing:

A little forgiveness

Step off a ledge and you still get your jump for a moment. Press jump just before you land and it fires as soon as you touch down. Missed inputs should never be why you lose.

Tap for a hop, hold for a full jump

A quick tap makes a small hop. Hold the button for the full arc, about 3.7 tiles high. Every gap in the game is built around those limits, so you never have to guess.

Chain your stomps

One pest is worth 100 points. Land on the next one before you touch the ground and the score doubles, up to 800. The risky route and the safe route are rarely the same.

Zoomies

Grab a bolt and Comet can't be hurt for a few seconds. Just long enough to run straight through the spikeshell that has been annoying you all level.

Hazards warn you first

Crumble planks shake before they fall. Geysers hiss before they erupt. Shardlings shimmer before their crystals light up. Nothing hurts you without a hint.

Touch, iPad or keyboard

Touch controls made for this game, a proper iPad layout instead of a stretched phone one, and keyboard support whenever you have one attached.

Three regions, thirty levels

Every region adds new hazards, keeps the old ones, and ends with a duel at the gate.

Levels 1 to 10

Greenward Coast

Sunny parks, moonlit caves and rooftop runs. Springs throw you seven tiles up, planks crumble underfoot, and hoppers arc at you the moment you commit to a jump. The Citadel Warden is waiting at the gate.

Meet the scuttlers, hoppers, spikeshells and buzzers.

Levels 11 to 20

Amber Highlands

A desert town under a wide, changing sky. Tumbleweeds roll with real gravity, off ledges, into pits and straight at you, while duneswoopers circle overhead and dive at wherever you are standing. From level 16 the ground erupts too.

Meet the tumbleweeds, duneswoopers and the Highland Alpha.

Levels 21 to 30

Starfall Reach

Frozen fields under an aurora. Shardlings carry star-crystals that flare on their own clock, so you can only stomp them while they are dark. Glimmermoths drift over and hover just above your head. Every older hazard comes back, colder.

Meet the shardlings, glimmermoths and the Starfall Tyrant.

The duels at each gate

You don't win these by out-jumping the boss. A BARK button appears, and the fight becomes about reading the charge instead of trading hits.

  1. Face the charge. Hold your ground and let it commit. If you back away, the pattern just resets.
  2. Bark, or sidestep. Bark once the charge is under way and it trips. Dodging so it slams into the arena wall works just as well, and takes more nerve.
  3. Stomp the head. You get 200 points a hit while it is stunned and 1500 when it drops. Then the gate falls and the doghouse is right there.

  • Highland AlphaAmber Highlands, 4 HP
  • Starfall TyrantStarfall Reach, final duel
